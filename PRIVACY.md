# Jocasta Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 5 October 2026*

Jocasta ("the Bot") is a bot operated by the staff of the Marvel Discord server ("the Server"). This policy describes what data the Bot accesses, why, and what happens to it.

## Data we process

The Bot only processes data as needed to provide its functionality, and only within the Server:

**Account information.** Usernames, display names, user IDs, avatars, server roles, and membership status. Used to verify permissions, assign roles (the opt-in news role and the temporary birthday role), and keep polls to one vote per member.

**Poll votes.** Votes are stored as user ID + chosen option so each member can vote once and change their vote. Votes are kept until the poll is deleted, and are removed on request.

**Message information.** Where relevant to a feature, the content and metadata of messages in designated channels are processed in real time (news relay, format enforcement, auto-replies, spoiler thread requests, moderator paging, etc). Message content is never retained by the Bot; where a record is needed, only anonymous identifiers (such as message IDs) are kept.

**Command interactions.** When you use the Bot's commands, the Bot processes your input, user ID, and roles solely to execute the command. Actions taken may be logged to Server staff channels.

## What we don't do

* We do not build user profiles or track users across servers.
* We do not sell, share, or transfer data to any third party.
* We do not use data to train machine learning or AI models.

## Retention

* Poll votes are kept until the poll is deleted, and removed on request.
* All other processing is transient; records of Bot actions exist as messages in Server staff channels, retained per the Server's moderation practices.
* The Bot's database is encrypted at rest.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or data the Bot has processed — including deletion requests — can be sent to **marvel@rocked03.dev**.
